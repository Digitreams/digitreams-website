Why remote works

Why UK businesses choose a remote, founder led agency.

Hiring locally is not the only way to get senior marketing help. Here is what changes when the people you meet are the people who do the work.

01Senior people only

Your campaigns are planned and managed by marketers with more than 15 years of experience across industries and markets. The strategy call, the build and the weekly optimisation all happen at senior level, because there is no junior layer to pass work down to.

02No hand offs after the sale

With many agencies, the person who wins your business disappears and an account manager takes over. At Digitreams the founders stay on your account. You message us directly, including on our UK WhatsApp number, and you get answers from the people running the ads.

03Lower overhead, honest pricing

We do not carry the cost of a large office or a long chain of account staff, and that saving shows in our starting prices. Management from $349 a month, AI UGC ads from $130 for three, and every figure converted into pounds on the pricing pages before you commit to anything.

04Judged on one number

Reach and impressions are easy to report and hard to bank. We report the figure that matters: your cost per qualified lead. Campaigns are planned on marketing frameworks, with customised copy for each audience, and we spend the time on structure, signals and tracking before a campaign goes live.

Working across time zones is simpler than most clients expect. Campaigns run around the clock on the platforms themselves, reporting lands in your inbox every week, and calls are booked at times that suit a UK working day. If something urgent comes up, WhatsApp reaches us directly on +44 7475 418972, and email reaches us at info@digitreams.com.

We also keep the commercial side straightforward. There are no lock in contracts: arrangements run month to month, so our work has to keep earning your budget. Ad spend is always paid by you directly to the platform and is never included in our management fee, which keeps every cost visible.
