FAQ

Questions US businesses ask us first.

Where is Digitreams based, and can you work with businesses outside Virginia?

Our US base is in Alexandria, Virginia, and we serve businesses across the United States. Everything is run remotely with scheduled calls and weekly written reports, so your location never slows the work down. The founders lead every account personally, wherever the client is.

How much do your services cost?

Published starting prices keep this simple: Meta Ads and Google Ads management starts at $349 per month, AI UGC video ads start at $130 for 3 ads, and Social Media Management starts at $149 for a 10-day package. Websites and SEO are quoted individually after a free audit. Ad spend is separate and is paid by you directly to the platform.

Am I locked into a long contract?

No. There are no lock-in contracts. Monthly services run as monthly retainers and one-time projects are paid per project. We would rather earn your next month with results and clear weekly reporting than hold you with paperwork.

How do you measure success?

On cost per qualified lead, and on return on ad spend for e-commerce. Clicks and impressions are reported, but they are never the headline. Every campaign is tracked end to end before launch, so the weekly report shows exactly what your spend produced.
