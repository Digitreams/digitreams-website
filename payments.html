Payments

Simple, documented payments: every engagement starts with an official invoice.

How paying works

Three steps, in this order, every time.

01

After your free audit, we send a written quote for the exact work.

02

You receive an official Digitreams invoice with the payment options and details on it.

03

Work begins as soon as your payment is confirmed.

Ways to pay

The option that applies to your engagement is printed on your invoice.

Wise

For international clients, payment goes to our Wise account shown on your invoice.

Bank transfer

Within Pakistan, pay by transfer to the bank account shown on your invoice.

Card payments

A card payment gateway is being added. Until then, your invoice carries the payment options available for your engagement.

Payment details are never published on this website. They appear only on your official invoice, so you always know exactly where your payment is going.

Stay safe when you pay

We never ask for card details by email, chat or phone. Pay only through the details given on your official Digitreams invoice.
