Privacy Policy

Last updated. 29 October 2026

This page explains, in plain English, what information Digitreams collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We keep it simple. We never sell your personal data.

01

What we collect

We only collect what we need to reply to you, run your audit, and deliver our services.

  • Details you give us. Your name, business name, email address, and phone or WhatsApp number, shared when you request a free audit or get in touch with us.
  • Website usage data. Pages you visit, how long you stay, and the device and browser you use, so we can see what is working on the site.
  • Advertising interaction data. Information from platforms such as Meta and Google about how people respond to our ads, including clicks and enquiries that start from an ad.

If you only browse the website and never contact us, we hold no name, email address, or phone number for you.

02

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiries.
  • To prepare and deliver your free audit.
  • To book and run audit calls with you.
  • To provide the services you have asked us for.
  • To improve our website and how it works.
  • To measure our advertising campaigns, so we know which ads bring real enquiries.

We do not use your details for anything unrelated to the reason you shared them.

03

What we never do

We never sell your personal data. Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone.

We also never rent or trade your details, and we never add you to a marketing list you did not ask to join.

04

Cookies and tracking

Our website may use tools such as Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel. These help us understand how people find and use the site, and let us measure whether our advertising is working.

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. You can block or delete them at any time in your browser settings. The website will still work, although our visit statistics become less accurate.

05

Sharing

We share personal data only with the service providers we need to run the business, and only as far as they need it to do their job.

  • Email services, so we can send and receive messages.
  • Hosting providers, so the website stays online.
  • Advertising platforms such as Meta and Google, so campaigns can be measured and improved.

We may also share information if the law requires us to. Beyond that, your details stay with us.

06

Your rights

Your data belongs to you. At any time, you can ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct anything that is wrong, or delete it completely.

Send your request to info@digitreams.com and we will act on it. There is no charge and no form to fill in.

07

Opting out

You can leave our marketing emails at any time. Use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email we send, or go straight to our Opt out page.

Opting out stops marketing emails only. If you are a client, we will still send the messages needed to deliver your service.
